[[reset-unit-implementation]]
===== SystemC/TLM-2.0 Implementation

`smc::reset_unit` is a loosely-timed TLM-2.0 model of the SMC reset
controller. Deglitch, extend, and powergood-stretch counters are
abstracted to zero latency. Per-clock-domain synchronisers are
pass-through. `b_transport` never calls `wait()`. DMI is never granted.

[source,cpp]
----
class reset_unit : public sc_core::sc_module
----

`reset_ctrl_t` is the per-subsystem bundle on `ss_reset_ctrl_o`.
`jtag_reset_ctrl` is applied through `set_jtag_ctrl()`.

====== Threads and sockets

`input_method` watches every input, tracks `ss_reset_complete_i`, and
detects the FLR rising edge. `flr_assert_method` / `flr_deassert_method`
pulse `flr_cool_n_`. `output_method` is the sole writer of every
`sc_out`. `start_of_simulation()` seeds edge trackers and posts the
first recompute.

Derived levels (post-JTAG, post-abstraction):

----
stable_cold_rst_n = powergood & cold_reset_n
rst_primary_n     = stable_cold_rst_n & rst_cool_ni & cool_from_flr_n
rst_core_n        = (rst_ext_wdt_ni & ~wdt_2nd_timeout) & rst_primary_n
                    & fuse_reset_n
rst_wdt_n         = rst_ext_wdt_ni & ~wdt_2nd_timeout
----

Primary-domain assert clears the register block (SS_* except FLR /
isolate). Cold-domain assert clears isolate / FLR counters, so those
survive a cool/FLR reset. `ISOLATE_REQ_PINEN_REG` clears on cold only
when the isolate pin is low.

FLR: rising `cfg_flr_pf_active_i` sets `ISOLATE_REQ_SMC_REG.bit0`. If
the reset-duration counter is non-zero, `rst_cool_no` asserts after
`FLR_COUNTER_VALUE × ref_clk_period_ns` and holds for
`FLR_RESET_COUNTER_VALUE × ref_clk_period_ns`.

`smc-vp` binds `reg_socket` on the peripheral router and wires every
reset pin to platform signals, including WDT first/second timeout from
the cluster.

====== CCI parameters

Subsystem count and ref-clock period are immutable. `access_delay_ns`
is re-read on every `b_transport`.

[cols="36%,20%,44%",options="header"]
|===
|Parameter |Default |Use
|`num_subsystems` |32 |Sizes `ss_reset_ctrl_o[]` (1..32)
|`ref_clk_period_ns` |10.0 |FLR counter time base
|`access_delay_ns` |2.0 |Annotated TLM access delay
|===

Out-of-range subsystem count or a negative period is `SC_REPORT_FATAL`.

====== Modeling gaps versus RTL

* Four-deep reset/data synchronisers are zero-delay pass-through.
* 32-cycle deglitch, 255-cycle extender, and powergood stretch are
  omitted.
* Scan / `test_mode` / `scan_rst_n` are not modelled.
* `smc_wdt_first_timeout_i` is informational and is not in the reset
  equation.
