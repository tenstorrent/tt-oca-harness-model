[[sep-scratch-cold]]
==== SEP Scratch Cold

The hardware contract (eight 64-bit scratch words, cold retention) is
in the hardware TRM. This directory documents the SystemC/TLM-2.0
model (`sep_scratch_cold_ip`) and how to test it. SIM_OUT and
SEP_STATUS decoding are VP conveniences on top of two of those words.
